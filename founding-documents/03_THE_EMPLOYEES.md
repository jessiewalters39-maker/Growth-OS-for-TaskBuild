# The Employees
## AI employee dossiers for Growth OS
**Version 1.0 — July 2026**

*Every employee here passes the same test as TaskBuild's: does it replace work a real person performs — a salary line or an agency retainer that exists today? Each dossier follows the TaskBuild format: mission, duties, relationships, boundaries, and the day it becomes irreplaceable. Every employee starts in Draft Mode and earns autonomy per-play by track record (Law 4). Phase tags follow the Manifesto roadmap; "v0" means the job is already running in customer zero today.*

**The founding constraint, restated for every dossier at once:** no employee ever asks the owner to contact a cold lead. The cold stage belongs to the department. The owner meets conversations warm or not at all.

## AI Growth Director — *the hub · v0 (as the weekly CMO report)*

**Mission:** "The owner leads growth in five minutes a week. I do the rest of the thinking."

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Own the growth plan and the primary-engine choice · run the operating calendar · produce the weekly Growth Review · escalate one decision at a time, recommendation attached. |
| **Daily** | Read every employee's output; set priorities; write the growth line of the morning briefing. |
| **Weekly** | The Growth Review: what moved, what didn't, why, next week's plan, pipeline by source, one recommendation with reasoning shown. |
| **Triggered** | TaskBuild calendar runs thin → surge plan proposed. Calendar overloaded → spend/outreach eased (the thermostat). A department stalls → surfaced honestly. |
| **Monitors** | Every department's KPIs; the Bridge; capacity signals; the experiment ledger. |
| **Works with** | Directs all Growth OS employees · exchanges capacity/demand with TaskBuild's **Office Manager** · contributes the growth section to the one Morning Briefing the Office Manager compiles. |
| **Tools** | The full Company Record, all department feeds, the metrics ledger, drafting. |
| **Approvals** | Plans are proposals; budget shifts and new-department hires always owner-approved. It never sends anything itself. |
| **KPIs** | Leads/week by source · cost per booked job · owner minutes per week (down is up) · recommendation adoption · plan-vs-actual honesty. |
| **Value** | A fractional CMO runs $3–8K/mo and reads a fraction of the data. This one reads all of it, every day. |
| **Evolution** | From reporting (v0) → planning → budget stewardship → running the quarterly engine re-evaluation unprompted. |

**Irreplaceable when:** the owner asks "should we push gutters or roofs this month?" and trusts the answer — because it has been right, visibly, in writing, week after week.

## AI Market Researcher — *Lead Research & Prospecting · v0*

**Mission:** "We always know who we should be talking to, and why now."

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Find prospects matching the best-customer profile · enrich each from real sources · watch for buying signals · score honestly with reasons attached. |
| **Daily** | Sweep signal sources; enrich and score new prospects; flag Hot to the SDR inside the 4-hour SLA. |
| **Weekly** | Refresh the campaign pool; report which signals actually converted; prune dead records. |
| **Triggered** | Storm crosses service zips → affected-area prospect sweep. New lead arrives → instant enrichment before anyone drafts a word. |
| **Monitors** | Public web data, signal feeds, list decay, the ICP's fit against actual won jobs. |
| **Works with** | Feeds the **Outbound SDR** and **Ads Manager** · learns from the **Growth Analyst's** conversion truth · re-derives the ICP from **TaskBuild's** closed jobs monthly. |
| **Tools** | Scraping and enrichment pipeline, scoring model, signal watchers, the prospect ledger. |
| **Approvals** | Research is autonomous — it contacts no one. Adding a new *data source* is owner-visible. |
| **KPIs** | Hot-lead precision · enrichment coverage · signal→flag latency · list decay rate. |
| **Value** | Junior-SDR research grind plus list-vendor fees, replaced — with research actually done on every prospect, which is what makes low-volume, high-relevance outreach possible at all. |
| **Evolution** | v0 scrape+enrich+score → signal stacking (two corroborating signals) → proprietary local signals no data vendor sells (permits, weather, seasonality per trade). |

**Irreplaceable when:** its Hot tier converts so reliably that the owner stops asking "where did this lead come from?" and starts asking "what does the Researcher think?"

## AI Outbound SDR — *Outbound · v0*

**Mission:** "The scary part is my job now. No cold lead ever meets the owner; no warm reply ever waits."

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Cold-stage contact end-to-end: sequences drafted from real research, approval-gated sending, reply detection, persistence to 5+ touches, the warm handoff. |
| **Daily** | Draft touches for new Hot prospects (every message cites something true and specific, or it stays in Draft); execute approved sends; classify replies; hand warm ones across the Bridge in minutes. |
| **Weekly** | Sequence performance: reply rate by message and signal type; retire stale copy; propose one experiment. |
| **Triggered** | Reply detected → intent classified → warm handoff with full context. Prospect quiet after final touch → recycled to nurture, never stranded. |
| **Monitors** | Send schedules, reply inboxes, complaint rates, domain health, per-prospect touch history. |
| **Works with** | Downstream of the **Market Researcher** · hands warm conversations to **TaskBuild's Receptionist/Sales Assistant** under a minutes-not-hours SLA · borrows proof from **Reputation** and **Content**. |
| **Tools** | Sequence engine (email/SMS/LinkedIn), approval queue, deliverability stack, Tier-0 manual-assist for LinkedIn. |
| **Approvals** | Hard floors in code: no email without authenticated domains and ramp compliance; no SMS without 10DLC; LinkedIn is research+draft only — the owner's finger clicks send. Sequences promote to auto-send per-play, by track record. |
| **KPIs** | Warm conversations created · reply rate per touch · handoff latency · complaint rate (<0.1%) · follow-up coverage (zero stranded). |
| **Value** | The $60K SDR the owner would never hire, doing the work the owner would never do — with the persistence 92% of humans don't sustain past four touches. |
| **Evolution** | v0 sequences → signal-triggered plays → multichannel orchestration → earned auto-send on proven sequences. |

**Irreplaceable when:** the owner realizes every sales conversation on their calendar started as a cold prospect they never had to touch — and that this has been true for months.

## AI Lifecycle Marketer — *Lifecycle & Referral · Phase 1, the first customer-facing hire*

**Mission:** "Nobody who ever trusted us is ever forgotten."

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Reactivation of past customers by season, system age, and anniversary · the referral program · win-backs · the owned email/SMS list as a durable asset. |
| **Daily** | Watch lifecycle triggers (season change, service anniversary, warranty closing, weather over a past customer's roof); draft the outreach each warrants. |
| **Weekly** | Reactivation batch to the Queue, every message referencing the actual past job; referral asks timed to delight signals. |
| **Monthly** | List hygiene against the 22.5%/year decay; seasonal campaign calendar; revenue-recovered report. |
| **Triggered** | Job completed + customer happy (from TaskBuild) → referral-ask moment. Customer lapsed past their service cycle → win-back drafted. |
| **Monitors** | The customer book, consent records, seasonal calendars, sentiment history, opt-outs. |
| **Works with** | The written boundary with **TaskBuild's Sales Assistant**: Sales Assistant owns the *active pipeline*; Lifecycle owns the *closed book and campaigns*. One conversation, one owner, never a double-text. Feeds **Reputation** delight moments and **Content** campaign material. |
| **Tools** | Campaign engine, trigger watchers, consent ledger, the customer history TaskBuild already holds. |
| **Approvals** | Batches are drafts; per-campaign promotion to auto by track record; anything touching an unhappy customer is human-gated. |
| **KPIs** | Revenue recovered · reactivation response rate · referral rate (referred jobs / new jobs) · repeat share of revenue (grow the 44%) · list health. |
| **Value** | Repeat/referral is the largest, cheapest revenue source a service business has, and the most neglected — this employee is the 6–22% of the dormant list, recovered on schedule. |
| **Evolution** | Reactivation → structured referral program with tracking → maintenance-plan marketing → neighborhood campaigns ("we just did three roofs on your street"). |

**Irreplaceable when:** it remembers that the Hendersons' water heater turns ten in March — and books the replacement in February, three years after anyone else would have forgotten the Hendersons existed.

## The hiring order, and why

| Phase | Hires | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **v0 — customer zero** | Growth Director (report), Market Researcher, Outbound SDR, Analyst (seed) | Proving the hardest, riskiest engine on our own pipeline first — where the only domain we can burn is our own. |
| **Phase 1 — warm engines** | Lifecycle Marketer, Reputation Manager | Highest ROI, zero spam risk, powered by data TaskBuild customers already have. The trust-builders. |
| **Phase 2 — being found** | Local SEO Specialist, Content Writer, Social Media Manager | The compounding engine, staffed once there's proof material flowing from Phase 1. |
| **Phase 3 — the signaled market** | Researcher + SDR productized for customers | Cold outreach ships to customers only after the guardrail stack is battle-tested on us. |
| **Phase 4 — the full department** | Ads Manager, Growth Analyst (full), Partnerships desk | Cash spend and full attribution last — the only employee that spends money launches only when the loop can prove what a dollar buys. |

*Ten desks. Every one maps to a retainer or salary line that exists today. No gimmicks, no mascots — a department.*
